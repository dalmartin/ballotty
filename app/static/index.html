Not affiliated with the Oregon Secretary of State, Lane, Benton or Multnomah County Elections, the Cities of Eugene, Corvallis or Portland, or any campaign. Summaries are machine-generated with Gemma 4 from official documents and can contain errors, so check the linked page before relying on one. Candidate statements are self-reported. Measure arguments are written by the people who filed them and were not verified by the counties. Ballotty does not recommend how to vote and does not rank candidates; they appear in ballot order.
